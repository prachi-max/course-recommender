How recommendations work
The short version
1
Every course becomes a vector. Title, skills, category, level, and description are combined and run through a TF-IDF vectorizer, so each course is a set of weighted keywords.
2
Your profile becomes a vector too. Your chosen interests/skills, plus the courses you've marked as known, are combined the same way.
3
Cosine similarity ranks every course against your profile vector — the closer the angle between them, the higher the match percentage.
4
This is content-based filtering: it only looks at what a course is about, never at what other users clicked. That's also why the "Because of: …" line can tell you exactly which words drove the match.
